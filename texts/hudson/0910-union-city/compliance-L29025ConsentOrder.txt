                    no later than July 30, 2026, to account for the collection and expenditure

                    of those funds.

                ii. The City should provide deed restrictions for all affordable units

                    constructed since the City’s Third Round settlement with FSHC.

                iii. The City should provide an updated Rehabilitation Manual for

                    rehabilitations funded through the City’s Housing Improvement Program.
