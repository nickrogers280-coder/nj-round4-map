14. The Town and FSHC recognize that substantial changes in circumstances affecting the

   Town's RDP are possible pursuant to the holding in Fair Share Housing Center v. Cherry

   Hill, 173 N.J. 393 (2002) and related law. In the event such a substantial changed

   circumstance occurs, the Town shall have one hundred twenty (120) days to present to the

   trial court and FSHC a plan to address such change in circumstances on notice and

   opportunity to be heard from FSHC. The Town agrees that any additional RDP generated

   due to changed circumstances must be addressed in a manner that is consistent with

   controlling law.

          fund as well as trust funds expended, including purposes and amounts of such

          expenditures, in the previous year from January 1st to December 31st.
