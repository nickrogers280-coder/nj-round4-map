       THIS MATTER, having come before the Court on referral from and recommendation

issued by the Affordable Housing Dispute Resolution Program (“Program”), pursuant to the

Complaint for Declaratory Judgment filed on January 29, 2025 (“DJ Complaint”) by the Petitioner,

TOWNSHIP OF WASHINGTON (“Petitioner” or “Municipality” or “Township”), pursuant to

N.J.S.A. 52:27D-304.2, -304.3, and -304.1(f)(1)(c) of the New Jersey Fair Housing Act, N.J.S.A.

52:27D-301, et seq. (collectively, the “FHA”), and in accordance with Section II.A of

Administrative Directive #14-24 (“Directive #14-24”) of the ”Program”, seeking a certification of

compliance with the FHA; and

       WHEREAS, the Court entered its Decision and Order Fixing Municipal Obligations for

“present need” and “prospective need” for the Fourth Round Housing Cycle on April 8, 2025 for

the Municipality – specifically, a “present need” obligation of 31 affordable housing units, and a

“prospective need” obligation of 152 affordable housing units (collectively, the “Fourth Round

Affordable Housing Obligation”); and

        IT IS on this 16th day of JULY 2026, HEREBY ADJUDGED AND ORDERED, as

 follows:

       1.      The Court readopts the findings and conclusions set forth in its February 27, 2026

 Approval Order as if set forth more fully herein.

       2.      The amended HEFSP, attachments, and implementing ordinances and resolutions

 described herein (including by reference to eCourts Trans ID #’s) are hereby admitted into

 evidence and entered into the record.

       3.      The Court finds and confirms that the Municipality has timely met all of its

 obligations under the Settlement, the Mediation Agreement, the FHA, and the Court’s February

 27, 2026 Order and Decision.

       4.      The Municipality is entitled to a Judgment of Compliance and Repose (“JOR”).

       5.      The Municipality’s Spending Plan, included as an exhibit to the Housing Element

 and Fair Share Plan, be, and hereby is also APPROVED.

       6.      That the JOR shall remain in full force and effect until July 1, 2035, and during

 this period, the Township and its Planning Board shall have repose from all Mount Laurel

 exclusionary zoning lawsuits, including, but not limited to, builders remedy lawsuits.

       7.      The Township shall continue to comply with the notice/update provisions of

 paragraph 5d of the Mediation Agreement.
