                                                                           4) DESCRIPTION OF ANTICIPATED USE OF AFFORDABLE HOUSING FUNDS
                                                                             (a) Rehabilitation and new construction programs and projects.
                                                                                None proposed.
                                                                             (b) Affordability Assistance (NJAC 5:99-2.5(a)).
                                                                                Municipalities are required to “set aside a portion of all development fees collected and interest earned for the purpose of providing
                                                                                affordability assistance to very-low, low-, and moderate-income households in affordable units included in the municipality’s fair
                                                                                share plan”. The regulations do not specify a certain percentage that must be attained, but for purposes of this document, a goal of
                                                                                20% has been set. The actual affordability assistance expenditures are calculated on an ongoing basis in the online Affordable
                                                                                Housing Monitoring System platform.
                                                                                 According to the chart on the following page, the Township of Holland has set a goal of 20% of all fees and interest earned to be
                                                                                 reserved for affordability assistance. This goal translates to $48,708.95. Through November 30, 2025 the Township has not spent
                                                                                 any funds on affordability assistance.
                                                                                 Holland proposes the following affordability assistance programs:
                                                                                       Supportive/Special Needs Units and Other Affordable Opportunities. Holland is located within the Highlands Region and
                                                                                        is a fully conforming municipality. The majority of the Township is served by wells and septics. Beyond the Huntington Knolls
                                                                                                                                                                                                          Page 4 of 8
                                                                                      site, opportunities for multi-family development do not exist. However, if the opportunity arises over the next decade to
                                                                                      partner with an entity to construct supportive / special needs affordable housing opportunities, Holland would like to reserve

                                                                                      well and septic.
                                                                                      It is anticipated that the program would be structured to provide funding per unit or bed in exchange for a deed restricted
                                                                                      unit / bed. A majority of these units would be very-low-income units.
                                                                                      In addition to supportive/special needs units, the Township would like the flexibility to use the trust fund for any other
                                                                                      affordable housing opportunities that arise. For example, to renovate an existing structure to provide affordable housing.
                                                                                      Up to $123,294.79 will be available for this mechanism, which is dependent on development fees collected.
