                                            TOWNSHIP OF RARITAN
                                       COUNTY OF HUNTERDON, NEW JERSEY


                                                RESOLUTION #25-6-135
    ENDORSING THE HOUSING ELEMENT AND FAIR SHARE PLAN ADOPTED BY THE RARITAN TOWNSHIP
    PLANNING BOARD IN CONNECTION WITH THE TOWNSHIP’S FOURTH ROUND AFFORDABLE HOUSING
                                       COMPLIANCE

WHEREAS, at the conclusion of a duly noticed public hearing on June 11, 2025, the Raritan Township Planning
Board (“Planning Board”) adopted a Housing Element and Fair Share Plan to the Township of Raritan’s Master Plan,
prepared by Jessica Caldwell, PP, AICP, LEED-GA, of J Caldwell & Associates, LLC, which was memorialized by
Resolution; and
WHEREAS, the Township Committee of the Township of Raritan has reviewed the adopted Housing Element and
Fair Share Plan, and agrees with the Planning Board’s finding that it complies with N.J.S.A. 40:55D-28, and the
amended Fair Housing Act, N.J.S.A. 52:27D-301, et seq. (“FHA”), in that it is designed to achieve the goal of
providing the realistic development of affordable housing to meet the Township’s present and prospective affordable
housing needs for the Fourth Round; and
WHEREAS, the Township Committee further agrees with the Planning Board’s determination that the Housing
Element and Fair Share Plan is consistent with the goals and objectives of the Township of Raritan’s Master Plan,
and that the adoption and implementation of the Housing Element and Fair Share Plan is in the public interest and
will protect public health and safety and promote the general welfare of the Township.
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED, by the Township Committee of the Township of Raritan, County of
Hunterdon, State of New Jersey, that the Township Committee hereby approves and endorses the Housing Element
and Fair Share Plan as adopted by the Planning Board on June 11, 2025.
BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that in accordance with the FHA and the Administrative Directive #14-24, dated
December 13, 2024, the Township Attorney and other appropriate Township official is hereby authorized to submit
the adopted and endorsed Housing Element and Fair Share Plan with the Affordable Housing Alternative Dispute
Program through the New Jersey Court system.
