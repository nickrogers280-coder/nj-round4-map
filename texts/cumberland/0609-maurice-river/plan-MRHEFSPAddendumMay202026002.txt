EXECUTIVE SUMMARY


       This addendum has been prepared to amend the 2025 HEFSP in accordance
with the filed “Consent Order Final Compliance Certificate” in the Matter of the
Application of the Township of Maurice River, County of Cumberland, Superior Court
of New Jersey Law Division, Cumberland County, Docket No. CUM-L-40-25.


       The Township of Maurice River adopted their 2025 Housing Element and Fair
Share Plan “HEFSP” on June 26, 2025. During the compliance review with the
Affordable Housing Dispute Resolution Program “(“the Program”) it determined that
the Transitional Housing credit proposed from the recently approved New
Beginnings Behavioral Health Center would be removed from the current plan and
reserved for possible inclusion at a later date. This amendment will provide the
Township with the ability to secure the appropriate crediting documentation which
cannot be completed until the facility is fully operational.

FAIR SHARE PLAN
       This addendum formally amends the Township’s mechanisms to address their
Fourth-Round Prospective Need.



Fourth Round Prospective Need:


       The Department of Community Affairs (DCA) for the State of New Jersey has
calculated proposed new affordable housing obligations for each municipality for
Round 4 (2025 through 2035). The Township’s Fourth-Round affordable housing
obligation is 8.


       The Township included one unit of credit for the recently approved New
Beginnings Behavioral Health facility as “Transitional Housing” in the adopted 2025
HEFSP. This facility has not been fully operational and is pending permitting,
licensing and construction. The Township is amending the Fourth Round Prospective
Need Plan to remove the transitional housing credit and reserves the right to
readdress the crediting mechanism once the facility is fully licensed and operational.


       The table in the 2025 HEFSP addressing the proposed Fourth-Round credits
will remove this unit and facility.


Elements Satisfying Obligation


New Beginnings Behavioral Health – 1 credit

       The 2024 amendments to the FHA included a new component for
“Transitional Housing” which can account for up to 10% of a Fourth-Round obligation.

The FHA defines “Transitional Housing,” which qualify for affordable housing credits.
The term means temporary housing which meets the following criteria:

       •   Includes, but is not limited to, single-room occupancy housing or shared living
           and supportive living arrangements;
       •   Provides access to on-site or off-site supportive services for very low-income
           households who have recently been homeless or lack stable housing;
       •   Is licensed by the department (DCA); and
       •   Allows households to remain for a minimum of six months.


       The Township Land Use Board approved a homeless shelter and licensed
inpatient behavioral health, drug and alcohol treatment facility on Block 220, Lot 5,
at 40 Schooner Landing Road. The facility is required to be licensed by the State
Department of Community Affairs. It is anticipated that at least one (1) bed from
the facility will address a portion of the Township’s Fourth-Round affordable
housing obligation. The Township is amending the HEFSP to remove this unit
of credit from the approved plan at this time and reserves the right to revisit
the crediting potential at a future date.
