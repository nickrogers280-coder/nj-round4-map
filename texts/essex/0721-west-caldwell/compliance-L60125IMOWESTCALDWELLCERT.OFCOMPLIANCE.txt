West Caldwell via counsel William E. Olson, Esq. (of Surenian, Edwards, Buzak & Nolan LLC),
as well as Fair Share Housing Center, via counsel Ariela Rutbeck-Goldman, Esq. (on behalf of

(“FHA”); and

(the “Program”) and a declaratory judgment action on January 23, 2025; and

complied with all terms outlined in the mediation agreement; and

implementing ordinances and resolutions, incorporating therein any changes from the Court’s prior

are met; and

   IT IS on this ______ day of __________________, 2026, ORDERED as follows:

1. The Township’s Fourth Round HEFSP, attachments, and applicable implementing
   ordinances and resolutions, collectively referenced at SCHEDULE-1 hereto as Exhibit P-
   1-4 (inclusive) and herein as the “Implementing Ordinances & Resolutions”, are hereby
   admitted into evidence and entered into the record.

2. Subject to the satisfaction of the Conditions of this Order and the deadlines established
   therein, the Township of West Caldwell’s Fourth Round Fair Share Plan (Exhibit P-1) is
   hereby approved and deemed to meet the “objective standard” pursuant to N.J.S.A.
   52:27D-304.1(f)(2)(b) of complying with the Fair Housing Act and the Mount Laurel
   doctrine and the Township is granted a Compliance Certification as to its Rehabilitation
   Obligation (“Present Need”), its Prior Round Obligation (1987-1999), its Third Round
   Obligation (consisting of both its Gap Obligation for 1999-2015 and its Prospective Need
   Obligation for 2015-2025), and its Fourth Round obligation pursuant to the Fair Housing
   Act (N.J.S.A. 52:27D-301, et. seq.) (“FHA”), the Uniform Housing Affordability Controls
   (N.J.A.C. 5:80-26.1, et seq.) (“UHAC”), applicable Council on Affordable Housing
   (hereinafter “COAH”) substantive rules, and Mount Laurel case law, including the New
   Jersey Supreme Court’s Mount Laurel IV decision.

3. The Township’s Compliance Certification shall remain in effect for ten (10) years
   beginning on July 1, 2025 and ending on June 30, 2035, and during this ten (10) year period
   the Township shall have repose from exclusionary zoning litigation, including, but not
   limited to, Builder’s Remedy lawsuits, as provided for in the New Jersey Fair Housing Act,
   N.J.S.A. 52:27D-301 et. seq. The Township’s Compliance Certification shall remain
   subject to prevailing law and rules of Court. The Court retains jurisdiction over this Order
   for purposes of enforcement.

4. As per the Township’s HEFSP and earlier court orders, the Township’s Present Need or
   Rehabilitation Obligation is 4, the Township’s Prior Round Obligation (1987-1999) is 200,
   the Township’s Third Round Obligation (1999-2025) is 406, and the Township’s Fourth
   Round Prospective Need (2025-2035) is 261.

5. The Township will address its Present Need via participation in the Essex County Home
   Improvement Program and a Township Program.

6. The Township’s Prior Round Obligation is 200 and previously received a Vacant Land
   Adjustment (“VLA”), which resulted in a Realistic Development Potential (“RDP”) of 18.
   The Township’s Prior Round has been satisfied by the mechanisms described on page 78
   of the Township’s Amended HEFSP.

7. The Township’s Third Round Obligation is 406. The Township previously received a
   VLA, and the Township’s Round 3 RDP pursuant to the October 31, 2024 Final Judgment
   of Compliance was 80 units. However, the Pio Costa Site, which generated 8 units of RDP
   (and was satisfied by rezoning the site for inclusionary zoning, including an 8-unit set
   aside), will be removed from the RDP and placed into unmet need resulting in a Round 3
   RDP of 72. The inclusionary zoning and opportunity for Pio Costa shall remain in place.
   The remainder of the Township’s RDP and unmet need credits are described on page 78 of
   the Amended HEFSP and include Heritage at West Caldwell, 45 Beechtree Road, Durkin,
   Greenwich House, and 75 Clinton Road as RDP mechanisms; as well as Caldwell Nursery,
   Rezoning of B-2, B-3, M-1, and M-2 Districts, Accordia, Pio Costa, and Township-Wide
   Set-Aside as unmet need mechanisms.


8. The Township’s Fourth Round Obligation is 261, which has been adjusted pursuant to a
   vacant land analysis to an RDP of 0.

9. The Township’s Round 4 Unmet Need mechanisms are as follows:
         a. A 4-bedroom group home at 138 Fairfield Ave. (ARC of Essex or a similarly
         qualified entity).
         b. A single-family home will be converted into 5-bedroom group home/supportive
         housing use at Block 705, Lot 7 (Community Options).
         c. New overlay zones as follows:
                 i. Block 2101, Lots 7 through 12 (Former ShopRite) has been overlaid at a
                 density of 20 units per acre, inclusive of a 20% set aside. The sites are
                 approximately 11.3 acres.
                 ii. Block 1200, Lot 3 Existing Office Complex, which is 7.01 acres
                 according to the tax card, has been overlaid at a density of 20 units per acre,
                 inclusive of a 20% set aside (a potential yield of 28 affordable units).
                         d. Accordia/Block 1201, Lot 3: 5 additional credits.

9. While the Township and FSHC disagree about the interpretation of the Amended Fair
Housing Act’s 25% requirement, pursuant to the mediation agreement referenced in Schedule-
1 as Exhibit P-2, the parties agree that the number is met through the mechanisms described in
the above paragraph and that the terms of that agreement shall control on this issue. The parties
agree that the language in this paragraph supersedes and replaces the language on page 67
through 68 of the Township’s Amended HEFSP, beginning at the language “The prospective
need obligation that has been adjusted, or RDP, is 0 for the Township” and ending at “While
the Township agrees to this measure as an instrument of settling, it should not be considered
an admission, nor waiver, of the Township’s position regarding the 25% language in the
Amended FHA.”
