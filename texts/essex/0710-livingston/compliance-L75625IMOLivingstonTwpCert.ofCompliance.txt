   of 40.5 units as follows:

       •   Esplanade/Westminster Hotel: 23 affordable family and age restricted units

       •   Livingston Mall: 31 affordable family and supportive housing units

       •   Gibbs College: 25 affordable family rental units

       •   Mark Built: 6 affordable family rental units

       •   389-405 East Mount Pleasant: 21 affordable age-restricted rental units

       •   Cedar Street Commons: 11 age-restricted rental units

and that the Township shall retain a 91-unit surplus.

10. The Township and FSHC agree that following conditions remain to be met as conditions

   of compliance certification:

       •   Within 60 days of the date of certificate compliance being granted, the Township

           will amend its Spending Plan to reflect that no more than 20% of the projected

           Fourth Round balance will be spent on administration.

11. The Township and FSHC recognize that substantial changes in circumstances affecting the

   Township's RDP are possible pursuant to the holding in Fair Share Housing Center v.

   Cherry Hill, 173 N.J. 393 (2002) and related law. In the event such a substantial changed

   circumstance occurs, the Township shall have one hundred twenty (120) days to present to

   the trial court and FSHC a plan to address such change in circumstances on notice and

   opportunity to be heard from FSHC. The Township agrees that any additional RDP

   generated due to changed circumstances must be addressed in a manner that is consistent

   with controlling law.
