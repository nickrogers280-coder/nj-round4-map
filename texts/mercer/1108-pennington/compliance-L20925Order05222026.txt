ORDERED as follows:



1.     The approved Fourth Round Housing Element and Fair Share Plan (“HEFSP”) (Exhibit

       P-1) and applicable ordinances and resolutions, collectively referenced herein as the

       “Implementing Ordinances and Resolutions,” are hereby entered into the record.

2.     The Borough has provided adequate notice of its proposed and adopted Implementing

       Ordinances & Resolutions for implementation of its approved Fourth Round HEFSP to the

       public and all interested parties in strict accordance with the Court’s prior orders and

       statutory requirements.

3.     The Borough’s approved Fourth Round HEFSP and constituent compliance mechanisms

       contained therein, and the Implementing Ordinances & Resolutions, are hereby determined

       to be fair and reasonable to the interests of the region’s low- and moderate-income

       households and to represent a realistic opportunity for the provision of affordable housing

       in accordance with the Mount Laurel doctrine, and specifically in accordance with the New

       Jersey Fair Housing Act, N.J.S.A. 52:27D-301, et seq. (the “FHA”), Administrative

       Directive #14-24, and the principles set forth in Morris County Fair Housing Council v.

       Boonton Twp., 197 N.J. Super. 359 (Law Div. 1984), aff’d o.b. 209 N.J. Super. 108 (App.

4.     The Borough’s Fourth Round HEFSP (Exhibit P-1) is hereby approved and deemed to

5.     The Borough is hereby GRANTED a final Compliance Certification as to its

       Rehabilitation obligation, its “Prior Round” obligation (1987-1999), its Third Round

       obligation (consisting of both its “Gap” obligation for 1999-2015 and its “Prospective

       Need” Obligation for 2015-2025), and its Fourth Round Obligation (consisting of both its

       “Present Need” and “Prospective Need” for 2025-2035) pursuant to the FHA, the Uniform

       Housing Affordability Controls, N.J.A.C. 5:80-26.1, et seq. (“UHAC”), applicable Council

       on Affordable Housing (“COAH”) substantive rules, and Mount Laurel case law, including

       the New Jersey Supreme Court’s decision in In re Adoption of N.J.A.C. 5:96 & 5:97 by

       N.J. Council on Affordable Housing, 221 N.J. 1 (2015) (“Mount Laurel IV”).

6.     The Borough and those parties implicated or otherwise involved and referenced therein are

       directed and ordered to proceed, forthwith, to implement the approved Fourth Round

       HEFSP and approved compliance mechanisms contained therein and in accordance with

       its terms and Implementing Ordinances & Resolutions.
