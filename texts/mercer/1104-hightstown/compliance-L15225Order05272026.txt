  Supreme Court’s Mount Laurel IV decision.

9. The Borough and FSHC recognize that substantial changes in

  circumstances         affecting       the     Borough's   RDP    are       possible

  pursuant to the holding in Fair Share Housing Center v.

  Cherry Hill, 173 N.J. 393, (2002) and related law. In the

  event such a substantial changed circumstance occurs, the

  Borough shall have one hundred twenty (120) days to present

  to the trial court and FSHC a plan to address such change

  in circumstances on notice and opportunity to be heard from

  FSHC. The Borough agrees that any additional RDP generated

  due to changed circumstances must be addressed in a manner

  that is consistent with controlling law.
