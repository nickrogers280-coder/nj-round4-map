  Mount Laurel doctrine and the Borough is granted a Compliance

  Round Obligation (consisting of both its Gap Obligation for

  1999-2015 and its Prospective Need Obligation for 2015-2025),

  and its Fourth Round obligation pursuant to the Fair Housing

  Act   (N.J.S.A.      52:27D-301,   et.   seq,)    ("FHA"),   the Uniform

  ("UHAC"),      applicable      Council     on      Affordable     Housing

  (hereinafter "COAH") substantive rules, and Mount Laurel case

  law, including the New Jersey Supreme Court's Mount Laurel IV

  decision.
