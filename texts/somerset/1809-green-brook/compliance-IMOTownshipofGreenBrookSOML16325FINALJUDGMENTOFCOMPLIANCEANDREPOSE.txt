        IT IS on this 15th day of JULY 2026, HEREBY ADJUDGED AND ORDERED, as

 follows:

       1.      The Court adopts the findings and conclusions set forth in its February 9, 2026

 Approval Order as if set forth more fully herein.

       2.      The amended HEFSP, attachments, and implementing ordinances and resolutions

       6.      That the JOR shall remain in full force and effect until July 1, 2035, and during

exclusionary zoning lawsuits, including, but not limited to, builders remedy lawsuits.
